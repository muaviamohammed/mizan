# The honest boundary

What MIZAN proves, and what it never claims.

**MIZAN proves named, measurable facts about the past.** A result, measured a stated way, counted against the tries registered before their outcomes, graded on the evidence ladder, checked against a bar fixed in advance.

**The count is a floor on a past record.** On a past record the count is what was registered: tries never registered cannot be counted. On a forward record, registered before any outcome exists, the count is complete from that point on.

**A rung is never inflated.** RECORDED is a record, not a proof. DECLARED never produces ALLOW. Public facts from outside sources are shown, never decisive.

**Unproven is not false.** `no_record` means nothing is proven yet.

**MIZAN never claims** the future, intent, or that any system is safe, aligned or honest in general. A credential is not a forecast and not advice about any payment; the relying party keeps responsibility.

**MIZAN never holds funds.** It does not custody, execute, route, allocate or release money.

**What is built but not yet released is labelled as such.** The status of each problem MIZAN addresses (live, partly live, building) is published at <https://mizan.market/problems/>.
